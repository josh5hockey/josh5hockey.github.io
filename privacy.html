Mac Mini Backups - Privacy Policy

Mac Mini Backups is a personal, noncommercial backup integration used exclusively by its owner.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth through rclone to access the owner's Google Drive account. This access is used to store, list, retrieve, and manage encrypted backup files.

Data Storage and Security

Backup contents are encrypted locally using Restic before being uploaded to Google Drive. Backup data is stored in the owner's Google Drive account and is not published through this website.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information, use advertising, or provide user data to advertisers. Google Drive processes data as the storage provider.

Data Retention and Deletion

The owner controls backup retention and deletion. Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Website Privacy

This website does not use advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies.
